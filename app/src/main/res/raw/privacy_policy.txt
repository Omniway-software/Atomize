2. Information We Collect
a) Account Information
When you register or log in with email and password, we collect:
- Your email address
- Encrypted password (we never store plain-text passwords)
This data is used solely to create and manage your account securely.

b) Analytics Data
We use Firebase Analytics to collect anonymous usage data, such as:
- Which features are used most often
- Device type and operating system
- App version and basic technical metrics
This helps us improve performance and user experience.

c) Crash Reports
We use Firebase Crashlytics to automatically collect crash logs and error reports.
These reports may include technical details such as:
- Device model and OS version
- Time and type of crash
- Anonymous session identifiers
This data is used only for debugging and improving app stability.

d) Payment Information
All subscriptions and payments are processed through the Google Play Store.
We do not collect or store your credit card or payment information.
Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information about payment processing.

e) Sensitive Data
We do not collect or process any sensitive personal information such as your location, contacts, or messages.

3. How We Use Your Information

- Analyze usage patterns and performance
- Fix bugs, crashes, and technical issues
- Provide customer support if you contact us
We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security
All user data is stored securely using Firebase (Google Cloud) infrastructure.
Passwords are encrypted and cannot be read by us.
We take reasonable measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.
However, no system is 100% secure. You use the App at your own risk.

We keep your data only as long as necessary to provide our services or as required by law.
If you delete your account, your personal data will be permanently removed from our systems within a reasonable time frame.
